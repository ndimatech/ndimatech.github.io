Common questions

What people usually ask first

Do you support both Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace?

Yes, both. We'll recommend whichever fits your business and budget better, and we stay independent either way.

How fast do you respond?

Support plans come with a written response commitment: next business evening on Essentials and Business plans, same-day on Priority. We promise only what we can deliver.

Are we locked into a contract?

You're free to leave anytime. Support plans are month-to-month with 30 days' notice. The service should earn its fee every month, and it's on us to make sure it does.

Outside Brakpan, can you still help?

Most support is remote, so we can help businesses across South Africa and neighbouring countries. On-site visits cover the East Rand and greater Johannesburg.

How much does a website cost?

Websites are fixed-price from R2,500, depending on what you need. After a quick chat you'll get a clear quote, an honest scope, and a bill that matches it.

What does the free IT health check involve?

A 20-minute review of your current setup: email, backups, passwords, security basics. We'll tell you honestly what needs attention and what can wait. The next step is entirely your call.
