What's billed separately

For full clarity, here's what sits outside the monthly fee:

  • Hardware purchases: sourced at cost plus a handling fee
  • Software licences: billed to you directly by Microsoft, Google or the vendor, at their standard price
  • Projects like migrations or office moves: quoted separately, at a fixed price. See Email & Cloud Migrations.

Common questions

Support plan FAQs

Do you support both Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace?

Yes, both.

What if we have an emergency outside your response window?

Priority-plan clients get same-day response. For genuine emergencies on other plans, an after-hours callout rate applies.

Are we locked into a contract?

Month-to-month with 30 days' notice. The service should earn its fee every month, and it's on us to make sure it does.

Outside Brakpan, can you still help?

Most support is remote, so we can help businesses across South Africa and neighbouring countries. On-site visits are limited to the East Rand and greater Johannesburg.

Still on old email?

Fix the foundation first

If your business is still running on outdated web-host email, a support plan is treating the symptom. A one-off migration to Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace fixes the root cause, and most migration clients move onto a support plan afterwards, with someone who already knows the setup.
