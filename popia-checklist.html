Free checklist for practices

Is your practice's IT POPIA-ready?

29 practical checks across 7 areas, written for small and medium medical, dental and legal practices. Work through it on your own or with your IT support provider. It takes about 20 minutes.

What's inside

Seven areas, 29 checks

Every check is something you can tick or not tick. No jargon, no 40-page policy pack.

  1. The paperwork the Regulator checksInformation Officer, PAIA manual and annual report, privacy notice.
  2. Email and accountsOwn-domain email, two-step verification, shared logins, leavers.
  3. Computers, phones and backupsEncryption, screen locks, updates, tested restores.
  4. Suppliers and cloud servicesOperator agreements and data stored outside South Africa.
  5. When something goes wrongThe 2025 eServices portal rule and a one-page incident plan.
  6. Reminders and marketing messagesWhat section 69 allows by email, SMS and WhatsApp.
  7. Keeping and deleting recordsRetention schedules, HPCSA guidance, wiping old computers.
