Privacy notice

How we handle your information

Last updated 18 November 2026

NdimaTech (Pty) Ltd ("NdimaTech", "we") is the responsible party for the personal information described here. This notice explains what we collect, why, who we share it with and how long we keep it, as required by the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Our Information Officer

Our Information Officer is the director of NdimaTech (Pty) Ltd. You can reach them at setup@ndimatech.co.za for any privacy question or request.

What we collect and why

WhenWhat we collectWhy
You ask for our free checklistEmail address; first name and practice type if you give them; whether you ticked the marketing box, and whenTo send you the checklist, and our tips and offers only if you ticked the box
You buy a product from us, such as the POPIA IT ToolkitYour name if you give it, email address, what you bought, the amount and a payment reference. We never see or store your card detailsTo take payment, email your download link, and keep the records tax law requires
You contact us by email or WhatsAppYour name, contact details and what you tell usTo reply and, if you ask, to quote for work
You become a clientContact and billing details, and the account and system details we need to do the workTo deliver our services, invoice you, and meet our legal and tax obligations

We only send marketing emails to people who have agreed to receive them. Every marketing email includes a link to unsubscribe.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your information. We use these service providers (operators) to run our business, and each processes your information only on our instructions:

  • Brevo stores our mailing list and sends the checklist, download links and our emails.
  • Yoco processes card payments for our products. Your card details go to Yoco, not to us.
  • Cloudflare runs our online shop, stores order records and delivers product downloads.
  • Google Workspace runs our business email.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and may log visitors' IP addresses for security.
  • WhatsApp carries any messages you send us there.

These providers store information on servers outside South Africa. We rely on their data processing terms, which protect your information in line with section 72 of POPIA.

How long we keep it

  • Checklist sign-ups without marketing consent: deleted 90 days after we send the checklist.
  • Mailing list subscribers: kept until you unsubscribe, then removed from our list.
  • Enquiries that don't lead to work: deleted after 12 months.
  • Product purchases: kept for 5 years, as tax law requires. Checkouts that were never paid are deleted after 14 days.
  • Client records and invoices: kept for 5 years after our work together ends, as tax law requires.

Your rights

You may ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Email setup@ndimatech.co.za and we'll respond within 30 days. You can also unsubscribe from marketing at any time using the link in our emails.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you may complain to the Information Regulator at inforegulator.org.za.

Security

We protect personal information with reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, and give access to it only to people who need it to do their work.
