Software & vendor support

Your business software, looked after end to end

Most businesses run on a handful of specialist systems: an accounting package, a booking or point-of-sale system, an industry platform, and the Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace account they all connect to. Each one has its own admin settings, its own vendor and its own support queue.

We take that on for you. We look after the admin side of your software, keep it properly connected to your email, and deal with your vendors on your behalf, so your team stays focused on the work that earns.

Our background

We know software support from the inside

Our background is in software support itself: triaging tickets, reproducing faults, reading browser and network logs, and escalating issues with the evidence a vendor support team needs to fix them first time.

That shapes how your issues get handled. A ticket that arrives with clear steps to reproduce, exact times, message references and screenshots skips the back-and-forth and gets resolved sooner. We write tickets the way vendor support teams hope to receive them.

Shipping & chartering offices

Built for offices that run on email

Shipbroking, chartering, operations and agency teams live in their inboxes. Many run a specialist email platform built for the shipping market, one that sits on top of Microsoft 365 and turns a flood of positions, orders and fixture mail into tagged, searchable work. We know how these platforms are put together, from the mailbox connection underneath to the tagging and workflows on top, so we can keep yours running the way your desks work.

Inside your shipping email platform

  • Mailboxes connected to the platform and syncing, with quick recovery when mail stops coming through
  • Tagging rules for vessels, cargoes, ports and counterparties, kept accurate as your book changes
  • Message tagging set up so every fixture's correspondence stays together, from pre-fixture through post-fixture
  • Teams, shared views and permissions, so each desk sees the right mailboxes and jobs
  • Templates, signatures and scheduled sends set up for how your brokers work
  • Attachment previews and exports investigated, with a practical workaround while a fix is on its way

We work alongside the platforms you already use. Everything runs through the admin access you grant us in your own accounts, and your contracts and vendor relationships stay exactly as they are.

Common questions

Software support FAQs

Which software can you look after?

Business software that has an admin area and a vendor support desk: accounting packages, booking and point-of-sale systems, CRMs, and industry platforms such as specialist email systems for shipping. We'll confirm what fits during the free look.

Do you know our specific software?

We're at home with Microsoft 365, Google Workspace and specialist email platforms built for shipping teams. For other specialist systems, such as voyage management or market data tools, our role is admin and vendor coordination, and we'll tell you upfront exactly where that line sits.

Do you replace our software or our vendor?

We work alongside them. Your licences, contracts and vendor relationships stay yours. We handle the admin, the follow-up and the joining-up between systems.

Do you need our passwords?

No. You add us as an admin user in your own accounts, so every change is logged under our name and you can remove access whenever you like.

Can you support offices anywhere in the world?

Yes. Software support is done remotely, so your location makes no difference to the service you get. Response times are agreed in writing before we start.
