Questions

Good to know

I'm behind on my returns. Can you still help?

Yes. We start by finding out exactly what's outstanding with SARS, then catch up in order of urgency and agree a plan with you.

Do I have to switch accounting software?

Not necessarily. We work with the common cloud packages and will only suggest a change if it saves you time or money.

How does the monthly fee work?

After the first meeting we send a written quote for the services you need. The fee stays the same each month unless the work changes, and we'll tell you before it does.

Can we meet in person?

Yes. Meet at our office, at your business, or on a video call, whichever suits you.
