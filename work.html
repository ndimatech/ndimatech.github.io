His Word Christian Centre

A church website in KwaThema, Springs, built to stay current every week without the church needing a developer, now with its own online shop.

  • ClientHis Word Christian Centre
  • ScopeWebsite, online shop, domain & DNS, Google setup, ongoing care
  • LaunchedOctober 2026
  • Live sitehiswordchristiancentre.co.za
hiswordchristiancentre.co.za

01What the church set out to do

The church already had its own domain and email, a homepage design in progress and an active YouTube channel for its sermons. The committee wanted to bring it all together in one welcoming place where members and visitors can find service times, watch recent messages, see upcoming events and learn how to give.

  • A site that works well on phones, where most members would see it
  • Sermons kept current every week, without technical skills
  • The church's existing email kept running through the switch

02What we built

We rebuilt the draft design into a fast, single-page site with About, Sermons, Ministries, Events, Plan a Visit and Give sections, using the committee's own words and photos.

  • Mobile-first design, with the latest sermon or series front and centre
  • Domain pointed to the new site with the email records left untouched, so no mail was lost
  • Secure HTTPS, Google Search Console and search-ready details, so the church can be found by name
  • Free hosting, so the church's running costs are just its domain and email

03How the weekly updates work

Everything that changes week to week sits in a few small content files, separate from the design, so an update can't break the layout.

  1. The church sends the new sermon's YouTube link.
  2. We add it with the correct title and date. Older messages move down on their own.
  3. The site rebuilds and the change is live in about a minute.

Every update is saved, so any change can be reversed, and the site stays online even if an update goes wrong. The church just sends the new link and we handle the rest.

Malhaya Beauty

A makeup, nails and hair business in KwaThema, Springs, where clients book a time and pay their deposit online, any time of day.

  • ClientMalhaya Beauty
  • ScopeWebsite, booking calendar, online payments, owner's app, domain & email, ongoing care
  • LaunchedOctober 2026
  • Live sitemalhayabeauty.co.za
malhayabeauty.co.za

01What the business set out to do

Malhaya Beauty was launching with makeup, nails and hair services, some at the studio and some at the client's home. The owner wanted bookings handled on the website itself, with a deposit paid upfront so that every slot in her diary is a committed one.

  • Clients choose a service, day and time and pay without a back-and-forth
  • Different days, hours and places for each kind of service
  • Payments, domain and hosting all in the owner's own name

02What we built

A custom website with its own booking system, built around how she actually works rather than a one-size-fits-all booking app.

  • A live calendar that shows only times she can really make, with travel time between home visits built in
  • The call-out fee worked out from the client's address: free nearby, a flat fee further out
  • Deposits and balances paid by card or Instant EFT straight into her account. A booking is only confirmed once the payment is verified
  • Confirmation and reminder emails for clients, and protection against spam and fake bookings

03How she runs it day to day

She manages everything from an app on her phone, signed in with Face ID. No laptop needed.

  1. A client books and pays a deposit on the website.
  2. Her phone gets a notification and an email with the booking details.
  3. From the app she can move bookings, block off time and message clients on WhatsApp in a tap.
  4. The day before, she gets a run sheet of her appointments.

We look after the site on a monthly care plan, so price changes, new services and fixes are handled for her.
